Ukraine
September 2023
Good yield outlook for summer crops,
but reduced production of grain maize

Throughout the summer of 2023, Ukraine experienced
predominantly favourable weather conditions, with above-
average rainfall in July. In most of the country, the yield
outlook for summer crops exceeds the 5 -year average by
more than 10%. The southern-central oblasts experienced
below-average rainfall in August, but this did not cause
severe damage to summer crops.
Winter and spring cereals benefited from favourable
spring weather conditions. The yield forecasts for wheat
and winter barley remain unchanged, i.e., above average
in most of the country. Spring barley benefited from
abundant rainfall during grain filling, leading to an upward
revision in yield forecasts for all oblasts.
The favourable conditions in July boosted the
development of summer crops. Y ield forecasts were
revised upwards for most of the country, except for the
southern and western oblasts. However, the summer crop
area is significantly reduced compared with the period
before Russia's invasion in Ukraine, particularly in oblasts
located in the conflict zones.
Despite the forecast increase in yield by 1 9%, we expect
that the production of grain maize at national level will be
reduced by 3% compared to the 2017-2021 average. The
areas cultivated with sunflowers and soybeans were less
affected than maize, and our production forecast
increased to 17% (sunflowers) and 30% (soybeans) above
the pre -war average. We estimate that of the total
production of summer crops 3 % of grain maize, 17% of
sunflowers, and 6% of soybeans remain in occupied
territory.

Meteorological overview
Warmer-than-usual conditions prevailed in the country, particularly in western parts and during August. A drier- than-
usual start to the summer (June) was followed by a wetter -than-usual July; and then a drier -than-usual August,
particularly in the second half of the month in eastern and central regions.

June
• Drier-than-usual conditions were observed in parts of eastern Ukraine ( Luhans’ka, Donets’ka) and most of central
Ukraine, with rainfall only 0 -50% of the long -term average (LTA) in most of Cherkas’ka , parts of Vinnyts’ka,
Kirovohrads’ka and Mykolayivs’ka, and in the northern and southernmost parts of Odes’ka oblast.
• Warmer-than-usual conditions, with daily average temperatures exceeding the LTA by between 0.5 °C and 2 °C,
were observed in most of Vinnyts’ka, as well as in southern Zhytomyrs’ka and parts of Cherkas’ka, Kirovohrads’ka,
Mikolayivs’ka and Odes’ka oblasts. Colder-than-usual conditions, with daily average temperatures down to between
0.5 °C and 2 ° C below the LTA, were observed in eastern Ukraine and in parts of L’vivs’ka , Ivano-Frankivs’ka and
Zakarpats’ka oblasts in the west.
