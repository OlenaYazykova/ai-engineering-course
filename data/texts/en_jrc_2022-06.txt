Reduced yield outlook for winter crops

Adverse weather conditions since the start of the 2021 –
2022 season have led to a poor yield outlook for winter
crops in a major part of central and western Ukraine. Fair
yields are expected in the east of the country.
After a difficult autumn, winter crops were further
affected by colder-than-usual temperatures during spring,
which, in central and western Ukraine, were combined with
a persistent rainfall deficit.
Our regional forecasts show a below -average yield
outlook for winter crops in central and western oblasts,
whereas fair yields are expected in the eastern half of the
country. At country level, our yield forecast is below the
historical trend and well below last year’s record level.
Currently above-average temperatures are expected, and
- depending on their magnitude – these could further
reduce the yield potential.
In terms of production, this translates to a forecast of
26.88 Mt of wheat, 6.66 Mt of barley, and 3.75 Mt of
rapeseed. About 22% of the forecast soft wheat
production, 19% of the forecast winter barley, and 13 %
of the forecast rapeseed productio n is in areas that are
currently subject to hostilities due to Russia’s war on
Ukraine. Hostilities have also led to a sharp decrease in
the area sown to spring and summer crops, which will lead
to a lower production of spring barley, grain maize,
sunflowers and soybeans.

Meteorological overview
To date the winter crop season has been characterised by significant temperature fluctuations. A colder-than-usual
autumn was followed by a warmer-than-usual winter, and by a predominantly colder -than-usual spring. Rainfall has
been below average, with the most distinct anomalies in the south-western parts of the country. Above-average rainfall
was registered only in December and April.
Autumn (Sept – Oct – Nov)
• Drier-than-usual conditions prevailed overall, with
rainfall from 30% to 50% below the 1991 -2021 long -
term average (LTA). October was particularly dry.
• The rain deficit was most pronounced in the south-
west, especially in the oblasts of Odesa, Vinnytsya and
Khmelnytskiy.
• Temperatures were on average up to 2 °C below the
LTA in most of the country, except in the north -western
part where slightly above -average temperatures
prevailed.
